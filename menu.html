Keep them delicious

Cake Pop Care

  • Store: Cake pops remain fresh at room temperature for 3 days, refrigerated for 2 weeks, or frozen for up to 3 months.
  • Protect: Keep cake pops away from direct sunlight, moisture, and heat.
  • Serve refrigerated: Take cake pops out 1–2 hours before serving.
  • Serve frozen: Leave cake pops out overnight to thaw.
