How the Ordering Process Works

From your first request to pickup, here is what to expect.

  1. 1

    Send Your Request

    Submit an order request through the website or by email.

  2. 2

    Receive Payment Details

    KT Sweets will send a payment link. Payment options include Cash App, Apple Pay, PayPal, cash, and check.

  3. 3

    Your Order Begins

    Work on your order starts once payment is received.

  4. 4

    Arrange Pickup

    When your order is complete, KT Sweets will contact you to arrange pickup.
